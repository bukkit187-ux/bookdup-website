How BookdUp Works

How clients receive their provider's number, how they opt in, and what happens before any appointment messages are sent.

How Clients Receive the Booking Number

Each BookdUp provider — for example, a barber — has their own dedicated text number. Clients receive this number directly from their provider: in person, by text, or through the provider's own business page or social media when they're ready to schedule an appointment.

BookdUp does not publicly list provider numbers or contact clients who haven't already been given a number directly by their provider.

What Clients Receive

When a client texts their provider's BookdUp number for the first time, they receive a disclosure message before any booking or informational content is sent. Here is an example of what that message looks like:

Via Text · Your Provider's BookdUp Number

[ProviderName] uses BookdUp for appointment reminders. Reply YES to receive texts, NO to opt out.

[ProviderName] usa BookdUp para recordatorios de citas. Responde SÍ para recibir mensajes, NO para no recibirlos.

Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to cancel.
Privacy Policy  ·  SMS Terms

BookdUp serves clients in both English and Spanish. The reply the client sends — YES or SÍ — determines which language their appointment messages will be sent in.

The Opt-In Process, Step by Step

  1. 1
    Client receives the number. The client gets their provider's dedicated BookdUp number directly from the provider — in person, by text, or via the provider's business page or social media.
  2. 2
    Client texts the number. The client texts that number to request an appointment.
  3. 3
    Disclosure is sent. BookdUp automatically replies with a disclosure of the SMS service and asks the client to reply YES — or SÍ in Spanish — to confirm they want to receive appointment texts.
  4. 4
    Explicit confirmation required. Only after the client replies YES or SÍ does BookdUp proceed with appointment scheduling. No booking-related or informational messages are sent before this step.
